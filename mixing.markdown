### Pricing and Timeline

Pricing: $25/min of audio

Timeline: several weeks depending on amount of songs, new mix + client notes
weekly, maximum 6 revisions

### Process & Hardware

Besides providing me with the multi-tracks themselves, please link me to any
previous work of yours and any reference tracks from other artists. Also please
let me know if you have any specific production requests, e.g. vocal reverb,
guitar panning, anything at all.

My hardware includes a pair of Kali Audio LP-UNFs and also Audio-Technica
ATH-M50x headphones. I work mostly in-box but do utilize Neve and Portastudio
preamps for saturation as needed/requested.
